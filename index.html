OUR EQUIPMENT



Tried and Tested

F45 is a system based on high intensity interval training. The range of equipment provided in this package is designed around the programming and is
an essential part of your studio setup.


A strong franchise requires uniformity and a standard look and design, whether you are in Australia or any of the international locations. Consistency
of branding and equipment allows F45 to differentiate ourselves from the competition and attaches a sense of quality in the mind of the consumer.


F45 have a ready-to-go supply of branded equipment, that can be shipped directly to your studio door.


The products within this range have been tested by F45 to ensure that they meet stringent requirements and industry best standards, however, it is
your responsibility to ensure correct installation and maintenance of the products, and inform your clients of proper equipment usage and handling.
We recommend that you as the franchisee conduct regular equipment audits to ensure that all your products are correctly maintained and replaced as
required.


This brochure also contains responses to many commonly asked questions so please take the time to read this information carefully.


F45 has compiled an equipment schedule based on years of experience – the equipment package is also constantly evolving and expanding based on
new workouts and developments. Individual items can be purchased as required if your products need to be refreshed or need to be replaced outside
of the warranty period.


View your Franchise Agreement for full details of your package.

TOP UP PACK EQUIPMENT



Mini Bands


The Mini Band, commonly referred to as Glute Bands or Booty Bands, is a versatile resistance band that can be used within a workout or as a warm-up.
Made from latex rubber and 550 mm / 21.6 inches in circumference, Mini Bands are designed to be worn above or below the knees to help activate
your hips, glutes and hamstrings, and target areas that would normally not be trained under a regular exercise regime.


The resistance of the band will be determined by the thickness of the latex - from black (hard), blue (medium) and red (low).


Usage: Bridge, clam shells, plank jacks, leg lifts, leg raises, resisted lunges, resisted kickback, squats.

Warranty: 3 months: is only against manufacturing fault (Delamination of the latex).

Lifespan: 6 months: should be inspected prior to each and every use for cuts or sign of wear and tear.
